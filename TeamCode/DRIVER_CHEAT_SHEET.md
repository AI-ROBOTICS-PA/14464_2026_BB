## Quick Reference Card (Print & Laminate!)

## ⚡ QUICK CONTROLS

| Button | Action |
|--------|--------|
| **A** | 🔄 Switch Intake ⟷ Launch |
| **B** | 🎯 Auto-Turret ON/OFF |
| **X** | 📐 Auto-Hood ON/OFF |
| **Y** | 🔥 Shooter Motor ON/OFF |
| **L1** | ⚡ FULL AUTO (all features) |
| **R1** | 💥 SHOOT 3 BALLS |

---

## 🏆 FAST CYCLE (20 sec)

### **COLLECT**
1. Press **A** (Intake mode)
2. Drive to balls
3. Auto-advances on detection

### **SHOOT**
1. Press **A** (Launch mode)
2. Press **L1** (Full auto)
3. Drive near goal
4. Press **R1** (Shoot!)

---

## 🎯 THE EASY WAY

```
1. Press L1 (Left Bumper)
   → Everything enabled!
   
2. Drive anywhere on field
   → Robot aims automatically
   
3. Press R1 (Right Bumper)
   → All 3 balls shoot!
```

---

## ⚠️ REMEMBER

- ✅ Forward = always "up field"
- ✅ Auto-turret = you drive, robot aims
- ✅ Must be in LAUNCH mode to shoot
- ✅ Wait 2 seconds for shooting sequence

---

## 🎮 DRIVE STICKS

```
Left Stick:  Forward/Back + Strafe
Right Stick: Rotate
```

Field-centric = Forward is always toward goal!
